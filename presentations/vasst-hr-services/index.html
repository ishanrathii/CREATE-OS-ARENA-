The Problem

For Companies

  • Recruiters spend hours manually screening hundreds of resumes
  • Resumes often don't accurately reflect a candidate's practical skills
  • Small and medium-sized businesses can't afford dedicated HR teams or expensive recruitment software
  • Poor hiring decisions lead to higher employee turnover

The Problem

For Students & Job Seekers

  • Freshers have limited work experience, making it difficult to stand out
  • Talented students from Tier-2 and Tier-3 colleges are often overlooked
  • Candidates don't know which skills they need to improve for specific jobs
  • Job searching is time-consuming and discouraging

Our Solution

VASST HR Services is an AI-powered HR platform that connects employers with verified talent.

Instead of relying only on resumes, we evaluate candidates based on:

  • Actual skills - not just claimed expertise
  • Aptitude - ability to learn and grow
  • Communication ability - soft skills assessment
  • Job readiness - practical preparation

Plus: Acts as a virtual HR department for startups and SMEs by automating recruitment, onboarding, and employee management.

How the Platform Works

Step 1: Company Registration

  • Post a job opening
  • Specify required skills, qualifications, experience, salary, and location

Step 2: Student Registration

  • Create professional profile
  • Add education, skills, projects, certifications, internship experience, resume

Skill Verification & Analysis

Step 3: AI Resume Analysis

  • Extract key information
  • Identify technical and soft skills
  • Generate Resume Score

Step 4: Verified Skill Assessments

  • Aptitude tests
  • Communication assessments
  • Role-specific skill tests
  • Generate Verified Skill Score

Matching & Interview Preparation

Step 5: AI Candidate Ranking

  • Compare resume quality, skill score, communication ability, and personality fit
  • Generate compatibility score (e.g., 92% match)
  • Help recruiters quickly identify most suitable applicants

Step 6: AI Mock Interview

  • Practice interviews before applying
  • AI asks role-specific questions
  • Provide constructive feedback

Recruitment to Onboarding

Step 7: Employer Dashboard

  • View ranked candidates
  • Filter applicants & compare profiles
  • Schedule interviews & track progress

Step 8: Employee Onboarding

  • Offer letter generation
  • Digital document submission
  • Onboarding checklists & training modules
